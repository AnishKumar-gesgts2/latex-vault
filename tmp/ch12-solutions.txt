12.3 The Dot Product
1.
A dot product accepts two vectors and returns a scalar; multiplication of a scalar and vector returns a
vector.
 (a) (a · b) · c is meaningless: its first dot-product input is a scalar.
 (b) (a · b)c is meaningful scalar multiplication, returning a vector.
 (c) |a|(b · c) is meaningful multiplication of two scalars, returning a scalar.
 (d) a · (b + c) is meaningful, returning a scalar.
 (e) a · b + c attempts to add a scalar to a vector and is meaningless.
  (f) |a| · (b + c) is meaningless as a dot product: |a| is scalar.
